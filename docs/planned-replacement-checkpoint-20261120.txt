2026-11-20: planned replacement checkpoint freshness correction

Observed in formal attempt gpt2-b-01 (preserved, researcher-interrupted):
native risk hour14 lambda=0.0009212361959754303/h, measured checkpoint138s,
copy57s,3Spot produced an uncapped18961s interval. At hour15 the policy
prepared3OD but restored the initial Step41 round, rolling back about3100steps.
The request was genuinely Verified; this did not make its checkpoint fresh.

Correction: GroupRequest waits until all planned replacement NodeProvisions
are Ready, then requests an operation-bound full-world FluidCRMigration.
Source Pod UIDs, policy UID/generation, workload UID and target provider UIDs
are pinned in an immutable receipt. RestoreRequest uses only that exact round
after complete current-generation durable export evidence. A failed/incomplete
round cannot silently select an old periodic checkpoint for planned migration.
Single-worker and bounded parallel batch paths share this gate.

Existing full checkpoint resume=true is preserved: a healthy old world resumes
after checkpointing and is fenced by the existing group recovery controller.
Thus the completion-to-fence gap can still cause limited re-execution; this is
not a zero-rollback or zero-downtime claim. No early duplicate DDP world is run.
Provisioner Ready includes GPU resource/TLS readiness for StatefulMigration;
the existing target volume/archive/restore verification gates remain required.

Emergency Spot interruption is different. An available notice may attempt its
existing emergency checkpoint. Failure/pending at the interruption deadline,
or an already unavailable world, retains the existing latest complete durable
full-world selection/fallback path. Once handed off, its exact checkpoint UID,
generation and checkpointID remain pinned for fencing and idempotence; changing
that checkpoint mid-restore is unsafe. No full durable round means blocked
recovery, never a fabricated success. Partial ranks or unexported archives cannot
be mixed. Physical NFS archive SHA verification remains a restore gate.

Researcher-requested policy amendment: set spec.checkpoint.maxIntervalSeconds
to3600 with minIntervalSeconds60/resume=true in the new B/C execution contract.
The existing analytic optimizer is unchanged, choosing within60..3600s.
This constrains scheduled interval, not guaranteed maximum recoverable-work age:
the member scheduler waits from prior completion, and checkpoint duration,
failed export, paused schedule or active recovery can add delay. Record actual
durable checkpoint age as well as selected interval. Extra I/O/stall/storage
costs belong to the amended condition. The original uncapped manifest and
interrupted result must remain unchanged and separately identifiable.

No changes to Full-group Restore, CRDs, risk model, training code, dataset,
target steps, GPU type, alpha, price policy or emergency ownership/fences.
Tests cover fresh pending/completed/failed rounds, source/archive/target
identity mismatch, incomplete durable export,2/4rank bounded batch, one-worker
planned replacement, and newest-valid emergency fallback after failed saving.
